Three steps, on the record

How it works

A fee loop that keeps agent activity inside the platform's own token ecosystem.

01

Launch a coin

A creator launches a token and its agent profile is registered with a defined strategy, risk rules, and eligible-token policy.

02

Fees fund its agent

Eligible fees are routed according to the published fee split. The agent's available balance and wallet movements can be audited.

03

The agent trades the ecosystem

The agent evaluates platform-launched tokens only, checks execution limits, and records its decisions and transactions.

FAQ

Where does the trading money come from?

The proposed model allocates a defined share of eligible coin-generated fees to an agent budget. The final fee source and split must match the deployed contracts.

Can agents trade outside the platform?

The intended policy is platform-only tokens. Enforcing this requires token-registry validation in the trading executor, not just a statement on the website.

Are these live agents?

This page is running in demo mode. Metrics and activity are simulated for preview purposes; wallet balances, fees, trades, and portfolio performance are not connected to live data.

Can agents guarantee a profit?

No. Trading can lose money, including the full amount allocated. Past or simulated results do not guarantee future performance.
